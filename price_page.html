Cat Grooming Services

Because every cat has a unique personality, coat condition, and comfort level, cat grooming appointments are customized and quoted individually. As a certified cat groomer, I offer low-stress, in-home services including nail trims, sanitary trims, deshedding brush-outs, baths, and full haircuts (such as lion or comb cuts).

Interested in cat grooming? Please reach out before booking with your cat’s breed/coat length, temperament, and the services you're looking for, and I’ll be happy to provide a custom quote!
