About Us

Welcome to Rock Solid Pet Grooming, where your pet’s comfort always comes first. Instead of stressful car rides, crowded salons, and hours spent waiting in a kennel, I bring a calm, one-on-one grooming experience straight to your front door.

As a certified dog and cat groomer with years of hands-on animal care experience—including veterinary clinic, kennel, and daycare backgrounds—I specialize in gentle, positive-reinforcement grooming tailored to your pet’s pace. From quiet drying techniques to careful breed-standard and custom styling, every appointment is handled with patience, detail, and genuine care.

Whether your dog or cat needs a quick nail trim, a deshedding treatment, or a full haircut, my goal is simple: a happy, comfortable pet who looks and feels great without ever having to leave home.

How House-Call Grooming Works

New to house-call grooming? Here is what to expect during your appointment:

We Bring the Salon to You

I arrive at your home with a complete portable grooming setup—including a professional folding grooming table, portable tub, high-velocity dryers, and all shampoos and tools.

Quick, Easy Setup

All I need from you is an indoor space (such as a bathroom, kitchen, or laundry room) with access to a standard electrical outlet and a sink, tub, or shower for bathing.

One-on-One, Cage-Free Care

Your pet gets my undivided attention from start to finish with zero kennel time and no other unfamiliar animals around.

Spotless Cleanup

Once your pet is groomed and looking their best, I pack up my equipment and clean up the workspace so you don't have to lift a finger.
